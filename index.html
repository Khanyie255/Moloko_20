FILE #07 / PERSONAL ARCHIVE
MOLOKO. 20

THERE ARE SOME THINGS I NEVER REALLY KNEW HOW TO SAY.

01

WHAT I'M GRATEFUL FOR

We've had our fair share of ups and downs, but one thing I will always be grateful for is that you were there during a year when I thought I would have to face everything alone.

You were there during a time when I thought I would be isolated after losing my mom. You also helped me find my way back to God and church, and I will always appreciate that part of our story.

Thank you for the laughs, the fun, and the moments where you genuinely considered my feelings.

02

THE MEMORIES

Some of my favourite memories will always be the simple ones. Going to church, the double date afterwards, your car stalling more times than it should have, laughing, listening to music, and you irritating me while I was being a cutie patootie and you were being a baddie for the pictures. 😭

I remember going back to your house for dinner, then coming back to my house before you eventually went home. I remember going to your rugby games and wanting to be there for you because you had been there for me too.

And of course, the drives are always my favourite, me predicting that you were going to win, and all the little moments that somehow became memories I still carry with me.

I also remember MD, going with you, being nervous about you meeting my mom, and thinking you looked so good. That memory will always mean something to me.

03

WATCHING YOU GROW

I've watched you change in ways you may not always notice. I've seen your anger come down a notch, even though there is still room for growth, and I've noticed how you're becoming more intentional about protecting your peace.

I hope you continue growing into someone who knows his worth without forgetting the worth of the people around him.

04

TWENTY

Turning 20 doesn't mean you suddenly have to stop being a kid, stop having fun, or become serious about everything. It doesn't mean you have to abandon the people you love.

It simply means you're entering a new chapter. You can still laugh, enjoy life, and love people deeply, but I hope you also learn that your choices affect the people around you. You don't have to hurt people just to accommodate your own feelings.

05

SOMETHING I NEVER SAID PROPERLY

Sometimes when certain things happen, I may attack you, become emotional, or start crying. But underneath all of that, there is something I wish you understood.

Once someone becomes part of your world, their feelings matter too.

Imagine your world is vibrant and pink. Your friends, family, and the people you love are like the trees, water, and air around you. If you keep burning things or throwing trash everywhere, people will eventually tell you that it hurts them.

And if you keep responding with, "It's my world, I can do whatever I want," eventually those people may start feeling like they don't matter in it.

I just hope you remember that caring about your own peace should never mean forgetting how your actions affect someone else.

06

IMPORTANT INFORMATION

For the record, I am still the better driver. 😌

I also have better taste in music, and this is not up for debate.

07

FINAL NOTE

More than anything, I hope you always remember that you are worth loving, and that you are capable of loving people deeply when you choose to.

Life makes finding genuine people difficult sometimes — whether they're friends, family, or someone you love — and when you find people who genuinely care about you, I hope you learn to hold onto them and treat them with the same care they give you.

I hope the years ahead bring you success, happiness, blessings, and people who remind you that you are enough.

And no matter where life takes us, I hope you remember that I will always be grateful for the memories we've made, the person you've been in my life, and the fact that our paths crossed in the first place.

Happy 20th, Moloko. ❤️
